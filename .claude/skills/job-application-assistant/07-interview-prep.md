# Interview Preparation Guide

Interview stories must come from the private, fact-checked candidate profile.

## STAR template

### [YOUR_STORY]

- Source evidence: [PRIVATE_EVIDENCE_REFERENCE]
- Situation: [SITUATION]
- Task: [TASK]
- Action: [ACTION]
- Result: [RESULT]
- Relevance to this JD: [MAPPED_REQUIREMENT]

Never ship a filled story in this tracked file. Never turn an approximate,
team-level, or unverified result into a personal achievement.

## Preparation loop

1. Select requirements from the target JD.
2. Map each question to a supported STAR story or mark the gap.
3. Research the company using saved source URLs.
4. Practice concise answers and one honest gap response.
5. Prepare role, team, success-measure, and culture questions.
