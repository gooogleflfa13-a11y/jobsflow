# Code of Conduct

We want JobsFlow to be useful and safe for people from every profession and
background.

Be respectful, assume good intent, critique work rather than people, and do not
publish another person's résumé, contact details, tracker data or application
materials. Harassment, discrimination, threats and deliberate privacy violations
are not acceptable.

Maintainers may edit or remove contributions and restrict participation when
needed to protect users or the community. Report conduct concerns privately
through the repository maintainer's profile or GitHub's reporting tools. Reports
will be handled as confidentially as reasonably possible.
